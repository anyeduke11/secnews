# Chainalysis 2025 Crypto Crime Report — Pig Butchering & Investment Scams (excerpt)

## Scale of Pig Butchering

Revenue flowing to pig butchering scam operations reached an estimated $9.9 billion in 2024, likely an underestimate given the private nature of many wallets. Scam platforms are long-lived: the average known scam token operation remains active for over 180 days, recycling infrastructure across victim cohorts.

## Infrastructure and Money Trail

Victim deposits land in hot wallets controlled by the scam platform, are consolidated into intermediary wallets, and then bridged or swapped through mixers and OTC brokers into fiat. Blockchain analysis shows distinct "hub-and-spoke" consolidation patterns that compliance teams can flag.

## Targeting Tactics

Operators run large-scale social media campaigns, impersonate attractive strangers, and coach victims through downloading custom apps that display fabricated balances. Requests for additional deposits spike before a coordinated "exit," after which withdrawals are disabled.

**Compliance takeaway**: exchanges and banks should screen deposit sources against known scam-cluster addresses, monitor for rapid consolidation into fresh deposit addresses, and apply enhanced due diligence when customers' stated purpose of transfers conflicts with on-chain counterparties.
